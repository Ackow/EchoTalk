# Job Description — Software Engineer (Backend), FinTech

## about the role [ai]

We are hiring a backend software engineer to join our payments platform team. You will design and operate services that process millions of transactions per day, working with a team of 8 engineers in a hybrid New York office.

## responsibilities [ai]

- Design, build, and operate high-availability payment APIs (Java / Go / Python)
- Own services end to end: design, implementation, testing, deployment, on-call
- Improve system reliability, observability, and latency at the p99 level
- Partner with product and risk teams to launch new payment features
- Mentor junior engineers through code review and pairing

## requirements [ai]

- 3+ years of backend experience building production services
- Solid grounding in data structures, algorithms, and distributed systems
- Hands-on experience with relational databases (MySQL or PostgreSQL) and message queues (Kafka or RabbitMQ)
- Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS / GCP) and containerized deployment (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Strong communication skills in English; able to explain trade-offs clearly

## nice to have [ai]

- Experience in payments, banking, or another regulated domain
- Familiarity with idempotency, reconciliation, and double-entry ledger design
- Experience with feature-flag driven releases and progressive rollouts

## interview process [ai]

1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
2. Online coding assessment (90 min)
3. Technical deep dive: system design (60 min)
4. Onsite: coding + architecture + behavioral (3 rounds)
5. Team-match and offer
